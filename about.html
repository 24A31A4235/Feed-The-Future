Who We Are

Feed The Future — restaurants share leftover food and their address, our workers collect it and serve it to people in need on the roadside. Every day, tons of food go to waste while millions of people go hungry. We aim to bridge this gap by collecting leftover meals from restaurants, events, and individuals and distributing them to the less fortunate. The site also lists subsidised government products at low prices.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: "No Food Should Go to Waste, No One Should Go Hungry." We work with restaurants, caterers, and volunteers to ensure that surplus food reaches those who need it most, rather than ending up in landfills.

How It Works

1. Restaurants share their leftover food and address on the site.
2. Our workers collect the food and ensure it is safe for consumption.
3. The food is then served to people in need on the roadside.
